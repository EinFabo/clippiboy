Save a clip, take a screenshot, switch the replay buffer and start a recording in ClippiBoy, straight from your Stream Deck. The keys show what is going on: how much is in the buffer, whether it runs, and how long a recording has been going.

Requires ClippiBoy, the free replay-buffer app for Windows 10 and 11 (clippiboy.com). The plugin finds it by itself: no port, no password, nothing to set up.

Four actions:
- Save Clip: writes the last seconds of your game to a clip. Each key can have its own length (for example 30 seconds on one key, 2:30 on another), and the key shows what a press will save.
- Screenshot: into ClippiBoy's folder and gallery.
- Replay Buffer: on and off. The key follows along when you switch the buffer by hotkey, from the tray or in the app.
- Recording: start and stop, with the running time on the key.

A check mark confirms that the clip, screenshot or recording was saved. A warning sign means it was not, so you know right away and not hours later. While ClippiBoy is closed, the keys show a quiet dash.

The plugin only talks to ClippiBoy on your own PC and sends nothing anywhere else. It only asks ClippiBoy for its status while one of its keys is on screen.

Keypad keys only. Windows only.
