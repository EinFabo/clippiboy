Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

In short

  • ClippiBoy runs on your PC. Recordings, clips, screenshots and settings stay there.
  • No ads, no cookies, no tracking across sites. The app sends one anonymous usage count a day, which you can switch off.
  • Apart from that count, only the friends feature talks to our server, and only once you sign in with Discord for it.
  • Clips sent to friends go straight from PC to PC and never touch our server.
  • Only a clip you share as a link is uploaded. It is deleted after five days.

This website

clippiboy.com is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). In front of it sits Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA), which provides the address and delivers the page. Both process the data technically required for a visit, such as IP address, time, requested page and browser identifier, and keep their own logs of it.

The site sets no cookies and loads no fonts or scripts from third-party servers. Only if the current version is not already baked into the page does your browser ask the GitHub API (api.github.com) for it.

The download buttons fetch the installer from GitHub through clippiboy.com/get/…. For each download we add one to a count per link and day, for example “tiktok1, October 11: 12 downloads”. Nothing else about the download is stored. The link's name is also put into the installer's file name, so the app can tell which link it came from (see below).

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in delivering the site securely and reliably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The app without signing in

Without signing in, ClippiBoy only connects to GitHub:

  • Update check: the app downloads the file with the latest version number and, for an update, the installer from GitHub Releases.
  • ffmpeg: on first start, the app downloads the ffmpeg program once from GitHub Releases.

GitHub sees your IP address when this happens. Apart from that and the usage statistics below, the app sends nothing. The diagnostic report tool only writes a text file to your desktop. Whether you pass it on is up to you.

Usage statistics

Once a day, the app sends a short message to our server (api.clippiboy.com) so we can see how many people use ClippiBoy, how they found it and which features matter. It contains:

  • a random installation number the app made up, which is not linked to you, your PC or a friends account,
  • the app version,
  • the name of the download link, if the installer's file name carried one, and your answer to “Where did you hear about ClippiBoy?” in the setup, if you gave one,
  • how many clips, screenshots and recordings were saved since the last message,
  • whether the console, friends, sending to a friend, share links, the clip editor or the Stream Deck were used (yes or no for each).

It contains no clips, file names, titles, games, names, friends or sign-in, and it is sent without your friends account even when you are signed in. Our server does not store your IP address with it. We only use these numbers as aggregated statistics. Each day's numbers and the downloads per link are kept for 400 days; an installation that has not sent anything for 400 days is deleted entirely.

You can switch this off at any time in the setup or under Settings → Behaviour → Send anonymous usage stats. The app then sends nothing more and deletes its installation number and counts; switching it on again starts with a new number. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how ClippiBoy is found and used, so we can improve it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Friends feature

The friends feature is optional. It runs through our server api.clippiboy.com on Cloudflare Workers. The database is located in Western Europe.

Signing in with Discord

You sign in through Discord (Discord, Inc., USA). We only request the identify scope, which gives us your Discord ID, username, display name and avatar. We do not see your email address, your Discord friends, servers or messages.

What is stored

  • Account: Discord ID, username, display name, avatar link, your friend code, whether you accept requests, time of your first sign-in.
  • Sign-ins: one session key per device, of which only a hash is kept, plus when it was created and last used. Signing out deletes it.
  • Friends: friendships, pending requests and who you have blocked.
  • Online status: while the app is running, it reports whether you are online or busy, your status text and, if “Show what I'm playing” is on, which game you are playing and since when. This only goes to friends you have accepted. What is stored is the last status and when you were last online. With “Appear offline”, friends see you as offline.

The app loads your friends' avatars directly from Discord (cdn.discordapp.com). Discord sees your IP address when this happens.

Cloudflare keeps logs of requests to the server, for example with time, address and errors. We only use them for debugging. Cloudflare deletes them after a few days.

Sending clips to friends

A clip goes directly from your PC to your friend's PC, encrypted, using the iroh library. While the connection is set up, our server only passes on the request and the connection details of both devices and stores none of it. The clip itself never reaches our server. If a clip has separate tracks (game, microphone, voice chat), the ones you can hear in it go along, so your friend can change the mix. Tracks you muted or turned all the way down stay on your PC.

To set up connections, iroh uses public services run by n0, Inc. (USA). Devices register a random device ID and their relay server there. If no direct connection works, the data, still encrypted, travels through an n0 relay server. n0 sees IP addresses but not the content.

Share links

When you choose Share as link on a clip, the app uploads that clip, if needed shrunk to under 50 MB, together with its title, game, tags, size and a preview picture. Unless you turn off Settings → Friends → Show my name on share links, the page also shows your Discord display name and avatar. It is stored in Cloudflare R2 and anyone with the link can watch it at clippiboy.com/c/… for five days. After that, the clip and its picture are deleted automatically. You can delete a link earlier from the clip's menu; deleting the clip in the app deletes its link too. To keep the weekly limit, we keep a record of when you created a link and how large the clip was for up to two weeks. Once a link is deleted or expired, that record no longer holds the clip, its title, game or tags.

Links are not listed anywhere and are not indexed by search engines. We don't look at shared clips unless one is reported. To report a clip, use Report it on its page, or write to privacy@clippiboy.com. A report stores the clip, the reason you picked and your note, if you wrote one. It does not store who you are or your IP address, and it is deleted after 30 days. A reported clip may be removed, and an account that breaks the rules may be suspended. A suspended account is signed out, cannot sign in or share links, and its links are taken down.

Legal basis and deletion

We process this data so the friends feature works the way you use it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). It stays stored until you delete your account. In the app under Settings → Friends → Delete account, your account, sign-ins, friendships, blocks, online status and share links are removed immediately.

Services outside the EU

GitHub, Cloudflare, Discord and n0 are US companies and may process data there. For these transfers they rely on their own safeguards, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. Details are in their privacy policies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. To do so, write to privacy@clippiboy.com. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
